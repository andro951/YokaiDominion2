# Asset provenance ledger

No downloaded third-party models, textures, audio, or game art are included in this checkpoint.

The planned initial arena, character proxies, material shaders, UI geometry, and synthesized sounds are created directly in this project's source. They are development assets, not final production character art. They do not claim to reproduce the user's original Vaelira reference image.

Dependencies:
- Godot Engine 4.7.2, MIT: https://godotengine.org/license/
- .NET runtime and SDK: Microsoft's distributed components retain their licenses; use official distributions.

Before any external asset is committed, add: exact asset name, author, source URL, acquired date, exact license text/reference, required attribution, redistribution permission, and any changes. A free download is not evidence of commercial or source-redistribution rights. No artwork from League of Legends, Dota, or RuneScape is permitted merely because those games inspire mechanics.
