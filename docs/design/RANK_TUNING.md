# Provisional ability-rank tuning

All four ability ranks now change a real gameplay value. This is prototype balance, not an assertion that Isaac approved exact W/E/R numbers.

Base cooldowns at ranks 1 through 5:
- Q: 4.2, 3.9, 3.6, 3.3, 3.0 seconds (existing design).
- W: 10, 9.5, 9, 8.5, 8 seconds.
- E: 12, 11.25, 10.5, 9.75, 9 seconds.
- R: 60, 56, 52, 48, 44 seconds.

Conventional CDR applies to all, capped at 50%. Attack speed additionally synchronizes Q/W only. Upgrading does not retroactively change a currently running cooldown. Natural mana regeneration retains the original rank-one reference demand; ranks do not silently grant more regeneration. Mana costs, damage and crowd-control duration are unchanged by rank in this first milestone.
